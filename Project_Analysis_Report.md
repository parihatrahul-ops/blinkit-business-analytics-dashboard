# 📊 Project Analysis Report: Blinkit Operations Dashboard

## Executive Summary

This project presents an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to analyze the operational performance, financial performance, and logistics profitability of Blinkit, a quick-commerce delivery platform.

Using a dataset of **50,000 delivery orders**, the dashboard transforms raw operational data into meaningful business insights through KPI cards, PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, advanced Excel formulas, and VBA automation. The dashboard enables users to monitor key performance indicators, evaluate operational efficiency, and identify areas for business improvement.

---

# 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was to build an executive-level dashboard that provides a comprehensive view of Blinkit's delivery operations while answering key business questions related to profitability, service quality, and operational efficiency.

---

# 📂 Dataset Overview

The dashboard is built using a dataset containing approximately **50,000 Blinkit delivery orders**.

### Dataset Includes

- Order Details
- Order Date & Time
- Delivery Hub
- Delivery Distance
- Order Value
- Delivery Fee
- Driver Payout
- Delivery Duration
- Customer Rating
- On-Time Delivery Status
- Net Profit

---

# 📌 Business Questions Addressed

This dashboard answers the following business questions:

- Which delivery hub performs the best?
- How do revenue and profit change throughout the year?
- Which delivery distance segments generate logistics losses?
- During which hours does delivery demand peak?
- How efficiently are delivery hubs meeting service level expectations?

# 📊 Analysis Performed

## 1️⃣ Hub Performance Analysis

**Objective**

Evaluate operational performance across different delivery hubs.

**Metrics**

- Total Orders
- On-Time Delivery Rate

**Business Insight**

The comparison highlights differences in operational efficiency across hubs, making it easier to identify high-performing and underperforming locations.

---

## 2️⃣ Monthly Revenue & Profit Trend

**Objective**

Monitor financial performance throughout the year.

**Metrics**

- Gross Revenue
- Net Profit

**Business Insight**

Monthly analysis provides visibility into revenue and profitability trends, enabling continuous financial performance monitoring.

---

## 3️⃣ Logistics Margin Analysis

**Objective**

Determine how delivery distance impacts logistics profitability.

**Metrics**

- Delivery Fee
- Driver Payout
- Net Logistics Margin

**Business Insight**

The analysis shows that shorter delivery distances generate positive logistics margins, while longer delivery distances gradually reduce profitability because driver payouts exceed customer delivery fees.

---

## 4️⃣ Peak Hour Operations Analysis

**Objective**

Analyze hourly delivery demand and service performance.

**Metrics**

- Total Orders
- On-Time Delivery Rate

**Business Insight**

The dashboard highlights peak delivery hours and helps evaluate whether service performance remains consistent during periods of high demand.

# 💼 Key Business Insights

- Operational performance varies across delivery hubs.
- Revenue and profit remain relatively consistent throughout the year.
- Logistics profitability decreases as delivery distance increases.
- Longer delivery distances contribute to logistics margin leakage.
- Peak delivery hours experience higher order volumes while maintaining relatively stable service performance.
